# Emerald Scrolls

## About
An app for notes and digital diary.
Emerald Scrolls is a digital diary designed for those who want a personal space to write, reflect, and store memories. With a clean and intuitive interface, you can easily record your daily thoughts — all with privacy and security.

🔒 Protect your notes with a password

Keep your entries safe with password protection. Your content is yours alone.

✨ Key features:

- Simple and user-friendly interface

- Automatic date-based organization

- Password protection

- Light and dark themes

- Works offline

- Multiple languages

- Contains unobtrusive ads

Whether you're journaling to unwind, organize your ideas, or build a writing habit, Emerald Scrolls is your personal space on your phone.
